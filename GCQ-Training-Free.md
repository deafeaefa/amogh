# GCQ: Measuring and Closing the Grounding Gap in Quantized Edge VLMs, Training-Free

*Working document for the ODI 2026 Proposals track (NeurIPS 2026 workshop; 5-page main paper, references and appendix excluded, double-blind). This italic line is document metadata — drop it when porting to the LaTeX template.*

**Abstract.** Vision-language models (VLMs) reach smartphones, robots, and embedded systems almost exclusively through post-training quantization — yet the capability edge deployment most depends on, precise spatial understanding, is the one nobody checks. Every major VLM quantization method is validated on VQA, OCR, and reasoning benchmarks alone; to our knowledge, no published work systematically measures what quantization does to *visual grounding* — the ability to localize exactly where an object is, not just describe what is present. The risk is real: under token pruning, localization is the first capability to collapse (95% VQA retention vs. 47% grounding), and isolated reports describe 4-bit VLMs that classify scenes correctly while consistently failing to localize them. Practitioners may be shipping broken spatial perception behind healthy model-card numbers. This proposal asks one question: how do we keep precise spatial understanding and visual grounding intact while a VLM is quantized? We answer in two parts: the first systematic measurement of this "grounding gap" — quantizing Qwen3-VL-2B across bit-widths and reporting grounding retention beside VQA retention — and one **training-free** fix: a forward-only probe finds which layers carry spatial precision, and only those stay at 8-bit (~6% extra memory) in an otherwise standard 4-bit pipeline. Because nothing is retrained, restoring grounding cannot silently trade away VQA or chat ability; general capability is a pre-registered constraint throughout. For deployment, the recipe is drop-in — no training data, no fine-tuning, compatible with existing GPTQ/AWQ toolchains — and doubles as an audit protocol for any quantized VLM. For research, the grounding-gap curves, per-layer spatial-sensitivity map, and detection-in-the-loop protocol establish capability-aware compression as a measurable problem, extending well beyond grounding.

## 1. Summary

Every major VLM post-training-quantization (PTQ) paper — MBQ (arXiv 2412.19509, CVPR 2025), Q-VLM (2410.08119, NeurIPS 2024), VLMQ (2508.03351), LUQ (2509.23729), SPEED-Q (2511.08914) — validates on VQA, OCR, chart, and reasoning benchmarks. With one incidental exception — GWQ (2411.00850) includes a single RefCOCO table to validate its general outlier-preserving method — none reports any localization metric, and none analyzes how localization retention compares to VQA retention or makes grounding a compression target. Meanwhile, the token-pruning literature has already shown that localization is the *first* capability to collapse under compression, with retention asymmetries as extreme as 95% VQA vs. 47% visual grounding at the same budget (Nüwa, 2602.02951; FEATHER, 2412.13180, ICCV 2025). Quantization is the dominant edge-compression technique, and its effect on detection is an unguarded blind spot. Since the point of putting a VLM on a device is usually *perception* — find the object, point at the thing, read the scene — practitioners may be shipping broken perception behind healthy VQA numbers on the model card.

This proposal has three parts, deliberately minimal:

1. **A measurement study (the centerpiece).** Quantize Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct across bit-widths and measure the "grounding gap": grounding retention vs. VQA retention, side by side, for the first time to our knowledge. (Throughout, *grounding* means referring-expression localization — emitting a correct box for a phrase; the held-out ODinW — Object Detection in the Wild — evaluation probes its transfer toward open-vocabulary detection.)
2. **One training-free intervention.** *Localization-sensitivity-guided mixed precision*: profile how much each LLM module's quantization damages grounding, then spend a small memory budget promoting the most grounding-sensitive modules to 8-bit while everything else stays at the aggressive width — a forward-only procedure and a greedy allocation rule, applied over a completely standard quantization pipeline.
3. **An evaluation-protocol claim.** Every selection decision in the compression pipeline is made on grounding metrics with VQA as a constraint, inverting standard practice — and compressed-VLM releases should report grounding on the model card.

We call the recipe **GCQ** (grounding-consistent quantization). One model, one intervention, one protocol — and **no training anywhere in the pipeline**: no fine-tune, no distillation, no learned allocator. This is a deliberate answer to the double bind of capability repair: a recovery fine-tune can restore grounding by quietly trading away VQA, hallucination robustness, and chat behavior (and even ordinary LoRA fine-tuning is known to disproportionately hurt grounding — Mini-InternVL, 2410.16261). A training-free pipeline cannot make that trade; general capability can move only through quantization noise and calibration shift, both of which we track with an explicit constraint. The measurement study is the minimum publishable unit; the intervention is the constructive answer; the protocol is the community-practice contribution.

## 2. Motivation: the measurement blind spot

**The asymmetry is documented — for pruning.** FEATHER (2412.13180) showed that visual-token pruning leaves most benchmarks intact while localization accuracy decays roughly linearly to zero, and that popular benchmarks fail to detect this because they require minimal grounding. Nüwa (2602.02951) quantified the asymmetry at 95% VQA retention vs. 47.2% grounding retention under 88.9% token reduction; LiteLVLM (2605.13178) and FocusUI (2601.03928) report the same fragility for pixel/UI grounding (figures from recent preprints; re-verified before submission). The mechanism is intuitive: emitting a correct box requires a preserved global spatial reference frame and fine positional detail; answering "what color is the dog" does not.

**For quantization, nobody has looked systematically.** The VLM PTQ literature cited above evaluates exclusively on VQA/OCR/chart/doc/reasoning. MBQ frames quantization sensitivity as a *modality* issue (vision vs. language tokens), not a *capability* issue — no existing method asks which layers carry *localization*. Direct evidence is two isolated datapoints pointing in opposite directions: GWQ's single incidental table shows near-lossless RefCOCO grounding at ~4.6 average bits *when the top-1% outlier weights stay FP16* — a hint that precision placement matters, consistent with our H2 — while an applied vehicle-damage paper (2608.02470) observed that a 4-bit GPTQ Qwen-VL 2B classifies damage correctly at 87.3% but "fails consistently" at localizing it. Nobody has mapped where between those two datapoints the truth lies. Separately, an empirical Qwen3 quantization study (2505.02214) shows Qwen3 LLMs degrade more than Llama-3 at ≤3 bits and that the smallest models suffer most — exactly the regime edge deployment lives in.

**Why this fits ODI.** This is not primarily a compression paper; it is a claim that the field's *evaluation practice* for on-device multimodal models is blind to the capability those deployments exist to provide. A measurement protocol that exposes the gap — and a training-free recipe any practitioner can apply the same day — is immediately useful to anyone shipping a quantized VLM.

## 3. Why Qwen3-VL-2B

Qwen3-VL (technical report 2511.21631) trains grounding as a headline capability (COCO, Objects365, OpenImages, RefCOCO/+/g) and emits coordinates as plain text in a normalized [0, 1000] system, typically `{"bbox_2d": [x1, y1, x2, y2], "label": "..."}`. Coordinates are ordinary tokens in the output string, which makes coordinate-focused sensitivity probing trivial to implement. The 2B Instruct model is edge-realistic — ~1.5 GB of weights at W4 (4-bit weights; we write WxAy for x-bit weights with y-bit activations), fitting Jetson Orin Nano 8GB-class devices (footprint noted for context; on-device latency benchmarking is deferred, §9) — and cheap to iterate on. The ecosystem (GPTQModel/AutoAWQ with per-module bit overrides, transformers, ms-swift grounding-format converters) means every baseline is downloadable and the two-tier allocation is implementable without touching kernels. Known low-bit fragility of the Qwen3 family (2505.02214) means there is real headroom for a method to matter. Following 2607.08029, the vision tower is kept at BF16/INT8 throughout; all aggressive quantization targets the LLM.

## 4. Method

### 4.1 Setup and data hygiene

Reference model = Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct at BF16; quantized model = the same model with quantized LLM weights. **D_probe**: ~512 RefCOCO-train samples in the chat template (image + "Locate the {expression}, output bbox_2d in JSON" + ground-truth answer), used only for sensitivity profiling (§4.3). **D_dev**: 1,000 RefCOCO-val samples, used *only* for selection — never for calibration or profiling. Reported RefCOCO-val numbers exclude the 1,000 D_dev samples, and the ODinW-13 evaluation set is never touched by calibration, profiling, or selection, so D_probe, D_dev, and all reported test data are strictly disjoint. Two further hygiene rules: RefCOCOg uses the umd split (the google split leaks images between train and val), and D_probe excludes any image appearing in the val/test splits of *any* RefCOCO variant, since the three variants draw on overlapping COCO images.

We write **C** for the set of coordinate-token positions in a teacher-forced target: the tokens covering the digits, commas, and brackets inside `bbox_2d` arrays, identified by a regex over the target string and mapped to token positions — a mapping that remains well-defined if the tokenizer merges multi-digit spans into single tokens.

### 4.2 The protocol: detection in the loop

Every choice a compression pipeline normally makes by perplexity or VQA — group size, bit budget, which modules get promoted — is made here by D_dev grounding metrics: referring-expression comprehension (REC) accuracy@IoU 0.5 plus mean generalized IoU (GIoU) against ground-truth boxes. General capability is tracked as a *constraint* — VQA and hallucination scores must stay within a pre-registered tolerance (1.5 points) of the same-method text-calibrated quantized baseline, checked by a paired test on a fixed ~5k-item VQAv2 subset and POPE (sized so the confidence interval is well inside the tolerance) — rather than as the objective. This inversion is itself one of the paper's claims: the field's selection signal has been blind to detection, and any group compressing a perception-oriented VLM can adopt this protocol without adopting anything else in the paper.

### 4.3 The intervention: localization-sensitivity-guided mixed precision

**Step 1 — profiling (forward-only).** Profiling runs in the same direction the allocator spends: start from the *fully quantized* model at the aggressive width (a standard text-calibrated GPTQ/AWQ checkpoint — the base PTQ pipeline is deliberately left untouched), then for each LLM module m — attention and MLP treated separately per transformer block, ~2×28 ≈ 56 modules on the 2B — promote *only* m to 8-bit (round-to-nearest for profiling, so the 56 configs need no per-config calibration; promoted modules in final models are requantized with GPTQ/AWQ at 8-bit) and score the *reduction* in grounding damage on D_probe with a decoding-free proxy:

s_m = KL_base − KL_(+m),  where KL_config = E_D_probe [ Σ_{t ∈ C} KL( p_ref(·|x, y_<t) ‖ p_config(·|x, y_<t) ) ]

i.e., how much promoting m recovers the fully-quantized model's coordinate-token distributions toward the BF16 reference. This measures exactly the marginal utility the greedy allocator consumes, at the deployment operating point — rather than the damage of demoting one module from an otherwise-BF16 model, which assumes additivity across modules and is measured at the wrong operating point. This is one forward pass per module per probe batch — a few GPU-hours in total. **The proxy is validated before it is trusted** — token-level agreement on coordinate tokens is known to correlate imperfectly with geometric error (2512.10554) — so s_m rankings are checked for rank correlation against decoded REC-accuracy *gains* from the same single-module promotions on a module subset, and if the correlation is poor, profiling falls back to direct decoded REC on a smaller probe (more compute, same design).

**Step 2 — allocation (greedy, two-tier).** Given an average-bits budget B, start with every module at the aggressive width and repeatedly promote the module with the highest **s_m per extra byte** to 8-bit until the budget is exhausted. Embeddings and lm_head stay at 8-bit always (standard practice — and they carry the digit vocabulary). The arithmetic is stated honestly: at B = 4.25 the budget promotes ~88M of the 2B's ~1.4B quantizable LLM parameters — about 7 attention modules, or 2–3 MLP modules, or a mix (attention ≈ 12.6M, MLP ≈ 37.8M parameters per block) — costing about +6% of the LLM's W4 weight memory (on the order of 50–60 MB) over uniform W4; B = 4.5 doubles both. The allocation therefore has only a handful of promotions of resolution: A2 compares allocation *policies* at matched memory, not fine-grained curves. Allocation is applied at the most aggressive width where H1's gap manifests (expected W4; otherwise W3 or W4A8, matching §6), with B ∈ {4.25, 4.5} over a W4 base and budgets restated to keep the promoted fraction at the same ~6–12% if the base drops to W3. Only two tiers (aggressive + 8-bit) are used, precisely so deployment stays feasible.

**Controls.** (i) Uniform W4; (ii) the *same* greedy procedure driven by VQA-loss sensitivity (KL on answer tokens of VQA probe samples, matched probe size) at equal memory; (iii) random promotion at equal memory. Control (ii) is the scientific heart of the paper: if grounding-driven and VQA-driven allocations differ and grounding-driven wins on detection at equal memory, the core claim — *localization sensitivity ≠ general sensitivity* — is established.

**Known gaps between proxy and deployment, and their mitigation.** Modules are profiled in isolation, ignoring interaction effects when all modules are quantized simultaneously; s_m is teacher-forced while real errors compound during free decoding; and profiling uses RTN while final models use GPTQ/AWQ. All three gaps are bounded by the same rule: no allocation is ever accepted on proxy numbers — every candidate configuration is evaluated end-to-end on decoded D_dev REC/GIoU, and the proxy only orders the search.

### 4.4 What we deliberately do not do

**No calibration-set changes** — the base PTQ uses the field's standard text calibration, so any grounding gain is attributable to the allocation alone (grounding-aware calibration is deferred, §9). **No training of any kind** — no recovery fine-tune, no distillation, no LoRA (a coordinate-weighted distillation recovery is the pre-registered escalation if the training-free ceiling proves too low, deferred to the full paper; §9). No learned bit allocator (greedy is enough). No new architecture or coordinate head, no tokenizer changes, no RL, no novel activation-quantization scheme (W4A8 is used off-the-shelf as a stress condition only). One model, one proxy, one allocation rule, one protocol. If a component does not survive its ablation, it gets cut, not patched.

## 5. Experimental plan

**Evaluation pipeline.** Each REC sample is one image plus one chat-template query — "Locate the {expression}, output its bbox_2d in JSON" — decoded greedily (≤64 new tokens). The emitted `{"bbox_2d": [...]}` is JSON-parsed with a regex fallback, mapped [0,1000] → pixels, and scored by IoU against the ground-truth box (correct at IoU ≥ 0.5; mean GIoU recorded). Unparseable outputs count as wrong, with the parse-failure rate reported separately — quantization can break the output format itself, and that failure mode should be visible, not folded into localization error. No anchors, confidence thresholds, or NMS: one query, one box, one IoU. The three RefCOCO variants stress different skills — RefCOCO allows spatial language (testA is people-heavy, testB other objects), RefCOCO+ bans spatial words (attribute-based grounding), RefCOCOg uses sentence-length descriptions. For ODinW-13, the query is one category per prompt ("locate every {class}, output all bbox_2d in JSON"), predictions are greedily IoU-matched to ground truth, and precision/recall@0.5 is reported. REC accuracy is additionally stratified by ground-truth box size (small/medium/large), where degradation is expected to concentrate.

**Quantization settings.** BF16 reference; W8A16; W4A16; W3A16 — each via RTN, GPTQ, and AWQ where applicable — W4A8 as a single harder stress condition; and mixed-precision GCQ at average bits B ∈ {4.25, 4.5}.

**Benchmarks.** Grounding (primary): RefCOCO/+/g REC accuracy@0.5 (val/testA/testB, with val excluding D_dev; parse JSON, map [0,1000] to pixels) and mean GIoU; a held-out ODinW-13 (Object Detection in the Wild) zero-shot subset as out-of-distribution grounding, never touched by calibration, profiling, or selection. General (constraint): VQAv2 subset + POPE. Headline quantity: retention ratios **R = metric_quantized / metric_BF16, computed on accuracy-type metrics only** — REC accuracy for R_grounding, VQAv2 accuracy for R_VQA. Because benchmarks have different chance floors (POPE's binary floor is 50%, VQA has a strong language-prior floor, REC's floor is ≈0), the headline figure uses floor-corrected retention **R′ = (S_quantized − S_floor)/(S_BF16 − S_floor)**, with each floor measured empirically by an image-blind pass (blank image, same prompts); raw R and absolute point deltas are reported alongside. GIoU is reported as absolute values and deltas only, since a ratio of a metric that can sit near zero or go negative is not meaningful.

**Figure 1 (planned):** the grounding-gap curves — R_grounding vs. R_VQA across bit-widths, per quantization method. **Figure 2 (planned):** the localization-sensitivity map — s_m by depth, attention vs. MLP, with VQA sensitivity overlaid (the H2 picture). **Table 1 (planned):** main results grid, method × setting × {REC acc@0.5, mean GIoU, VQAv2, POPE, ODinW, weight memory}.

**Baselines and matched controls.** BF16; RTN/GPTQ/AWQ with standard text calibration (the field's status quo); uniform W4; VQA-sensitivity-driven allocation at equal memory; random promotion at equal memory; GCQ (grounding-driven allocation over the same standard-calibrated base). Equal-memory controls ensure any GCQ gain is attributable to *where* the bits go, not to how many there are.

**Ablations.** A1: sensitivity structure — Spearman ρ between s_m and per-module VQA sensitivity; do the two allocations actually differ? A2: allocation policy — grounding-driven vs. uniform vs. random vs. VQA-driven at matched average bits. A3: generalization — held-out ODinW-13 for an allocation chosen on RefCOCO (guards against phrasing overfit).

## 6. Hypotheses and pre-registered kill criteria

- **H1 (measurement).** At W4 — and clearly at W3 and W4A8 — grounding retention trails VQA retention by a meaningful margin (target: >10 percentage points of floor-corrected retention). Support: the pruning asymmetry (2602.02951, 2412.13180), the 4-bit localization-failure anecdote (2608.02470), Qwen3's documented low-bit fragility (2505.02214). GWQ's near-lossless RefCOCO at ~4.6 bits with FP16 outliers retained (2411.00850) tempers expectations at moderate bits — if the gap is real, it should open at uniform W4 and below, which is exactly where our grid concentrates.
- **H2 (structure).** Localization sensitivity s_m is non-uniform across modules and only partially correlated with VQA sensitivity (ρ noticeably < 1), so a grounding-driven allocation differs materially from a VQA-driven one.
- **H3 (method).** At the most aggressive setting where H1's gap manifests (expected W4; otherwise W3 or W4A8), grounding-driven allocation at B ≤ 4.5 recovers ≥90% of the BF16 grounding lost by the uniform baseline (same quantizer, same standard calibration), within the pre-registered 1.5-point VQA tolerance of that baseline, at the stated ~6–12% premium on LLM weight memory.

**Kill criteria (pre-registered honesty).** If grounding is essentially lossless at W4A16, the headline gap claim and the intervention move to the most aggressive settings already in the grid (W3A16, W4A8); if grounding is lossless *everywhere practical*, the paper pivots to a clean negative/contrast result — "weight quantization spares grounding; token pruning destroys it" — still a publishable measurement study. If H2 fails (sensitivity diffuse, or identical to VQA sensitivity), we report that structural null, fall back to a fixed always-protect list (embeddings, lm_head, first/last blocks), and the paper rides on the measurement study, which does not depend on H2. If the proxy fails its validation (poor rank correlation with decoded REC drops), profiling switches to direct decoded REC on a smaller probe. If the full training-free recipe cannot close the gap, we report the recovery ceiling honestly — the pre-registered escalation is a coordinate-weighted distillation recovery fine-tune, deferred to the full paper (§9). The measurement study (H1, weeks 1–4) is the minimum publishable unit on its own.

**Known limitations, stated up front.** (i) s_m is teacher-forced and profiled per-module in isolation; both are gaps between proxy and deployed behavior, mitigated as in §4.4 — the proxy is validated against decoded metrics before use, and every accepted configuration is selected end-to-end on decoded REC/GIoU. (ii) The novelty claim — that no published work *systematically* measures grounding degradation under low-bit quantization of a generative VLM or allocates quantization precision by localization sensitivity — is *to our knowledge*, a negative claim from a finite search; the closest lines are GWQ's single incidental RefCOCO table for method validation (2411.00850 — one model family, one bit setting, no retention analysis, nothing grounding-aware), modality-balanced PTQ (2412.19509), grounding-aware *pruning* (2412.13180, 2602.02951), quantization-aware protection of visual *input* tokens for VLA policies (2509.09090), and component-wise (vision/LLM) mixed precision evaluated on MME only (2607.08029). We will re-verify immediately before submission and camera-ready. (iii) Several motivating figures come from recent preprints and are treated as provisional.

## 7. Feasibility and timeline

**Everything in this proposal is forward-only** — quantization, profiling, allocation, and evaluation; there is no training run anywhere. Profiling is ~56 configs × 512 probe samples (a few GPU-hours); allocation is arithmetic; the dominant cost is decoded REC evaluation, which runs subset-first — 1–2k-sample REC subsets with cached image features, with only selected configurations evaluated on full splits. The full project fits in ~60–75 GPU-hours on a single 24 GB GPU — under $150 of cloud time at spot prices — with the deadline-critical slice (harness, baselines, and the measurement grid) needing only ~25–35 of those hours. Because the pipeline is forward-only, preliminary evidence — a first W4 grounding-gap measurement and a first sensitivity map — is obtainable within days and is planned for inclusion in this submission. All data is public (RefCOCO/+/g, ODinW, VQAv2, POPE); the ms-swift grounding converter handles [0,1000] normalization for Qwen3-VL. Team: a single researcher.

**4-week plan.** Week 1: evaluation harness (REC parser, GIoU, VQA/POPE subsets), BF16 + image-blind floor baselines, first W4 checkpoint → the first grounding-gap datapoint. Week 2: the full measurement grid on frozen subsets → Figure 1 (H1 resolved here; kill criteria applied here). Week 3: sensitivity profiling + proxy validation against decoded REC (kill criterion applied here) → Figure 2; greedy allocation and the first GCQ checkpoint. Week 4: equal-memory controls (A1, A2), held-out ODinW evaluation (A3), full-split runs for headline configs, write-up, scooping re-check, code and checkpoint release.

## 8. Deliverables

(1) The grounding-gap curves and tables — to our knowledge the first *systematic* quantization-vs-grounding measurement for a generative VLM. (2) The localization-sensitivity map of Qwen3-VL-2B — which layers carry grounding under quantization — as a standalone measurement artifact. (3) The training-free recipe with code: sensitivity profiler and greedy allocator as thin wrappers over GPTQModel/AutoAWQ. (4) One released mixed-precision quantized Qwen3-VL-2B checkpoint *with grounding numbers on the model card* — itself a small statement about how compressed VLMs should be reported. (5) A short written recommendation: compressed-VLM releases intended for perception workloads should report REC/GIoU retention alongside VQA.

## 9. Deferred to future work

A full paper following this proposal would add: **grounding-calibrated PTQ** — swapping the quantizer's calibration set for grounding data, with a three-arm attribution ablation (text-only vs. same-image captioning vs. grounding calibration); **the recovery fine-tune** — a coordinate-weighted distillation QLoRA in the spirit of localization distillation (2102.12252), the pre-registered escalation if the training-free ceiling proves too low, with the matched-compute controls and anti-forgetting constraints that training reintroduces; the 4B/8B models (does the gap shrink with scale?); a prompted-detection COCO AP pipeline including size-stratified AP; broader general benchmarks (GQA, MME, TextVQA); finer bit-budget Pareto sweeps and FP8/MBQ baselines; and on-device (Jetson-class) latency, memory, and throughput measurements with mixed-precision export (GGUF per-tensor overrides / vLLM compressed-tensors). None of these are needed to establish the blind spot or to test the training-free recipe, so all are out of scope here.
