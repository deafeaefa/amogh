\begin{abstract}
Vision-language models (VLMs) reach smartphones, robots, and embedded systems almost exclusively through post-training quantization---yet the capability edge deployment most depends on, precise spatial understanding, is the one nobody checks. Every major VLM quantization method is validated on VQA, OCR, and reasoning benchmarks alone; to our knowledge, no published work systematically measures what quantization does to \emph{visual grounding}---the ability to localize exactly where an object is, not just describe what is present. The risk is real: under token pruning localization collapses first (95\% VQA vs.\ 47\% grounding retention). This proposal asks one question: how do we keep precise spatial understanding and visual grounding intact while a VLM is quantized? We answer in two parts: the first systematic measurement of this ``grounding gap''---quantizing Qwen3-VL-2B across bit-widths and reporting grounding retention beside VQA retention---and one \textbf{training-free} fix: a forward-only probe finds which layers carry spatial precision, and only those stay at 8-bit ($\sim$6\% extra memory) in an otherwise standard 4-bit pipeline. Because nothing is retrained, restoring grounding cannot silently trade away VQA or chat ability; general capability is a pre-registered constraint throughout. The recipe is drop-in for existing quantization toolchains and doubles as an audit protocol for any quantized VLM; the released sensitivity maps and detection-in-the-loop protocol establish capability-aware compression as a measurable problem extending well beyond grounding.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Every major VLM post-training-quantization (PTQ) paper---MBQ~\cite{mbq}, Q-VLM~\cite{qvlm}, VLMQ~\cite{vlmq}, LUQ~\cite{luq}, SPEED-Q~\cite{speedq}---validates on VQA, OCR, chart, and reasoning benchmarks. With one incidental exception---GWQ~\cite{gwq} includes a single RefCOCO table to validate its general outlier-preserving method---none reports any localization metric, and none analyzes how localization retention compares to VQA retention or makes grounding a compression target. Meanwhile, the token-pruning literature has already shown that localization is the \emph{first} capability to collapse under compression, with retention asymmetries as extreme as 95\% VQA vs.\ 47\% visual grounding at the same budget~\cite{nuwa,feather}. Quantization is the dominant edge-compression technique, and its effect on detection is an unguarded blind spot. Since on-device deployment usually exists for \emph{perception}, practitioners may be shipping broken spatial understanding behind healthy VQA numbers on the model card.

That is a threefold gap --- no systematic \emph{measurement} of grounding under quantization, no \emph{method} that allocates precision by capability, and no \emph{reporting practice} that would expose the damage --- and this proposal answers each part in turn:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{A measurement study (the centerpiece).} Quantize Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct across bit-widths and measure the \emph{grounding gap}: grounding retention vs.\ VQA retention, side by side, for the first time to our knowledge. (\emph{Grounding} = referring-expression localization; a held-out ODinW evaluation probes transfer toward open-vocabulary detection.)
  \item \textbf{One training-free intervention.} \emph{Localization-sensitivity-guided mixed precision}: profile how much each module's quantization damages grounding, then promote the most grounding-sensitive modules to 8-bit under a small memory budget---forward-only, greedy, over a completely standard quantization pipeline.
  \item \textbf{An evaluation-protocol claim.} Every pipeline selection is made on grounding metrics with VQA as a constraint, inverting standard practice---and compressed-VLM releases should report grounding on the model card.
\end{enumerate}

We call the recipe \textbf{GCQ} (grounding-consistent quantization): one model, one intervention, one protocol---and no training anywhere. Training-free by design: a fine-tune can restore grounding by quietly trading away VQA (LoRA alone hurts grounding~\cite{miniinternvl}); a training-free pipeline cannot, and its residual channels are tracked by an explicit constraint.

\section{Background and related work}

\paragraph{The asymmetry is documented---for pruning.} FEATHER~\cite{feather} showed that visual-token pruning leaves most benchmarks intact while localization accuracy decays roughly linearly to zero, and that popular benchmarks fail to detect this because they require minimal grounding. N\"uwa~\cite{nuwa} quantified the asymmetry at 95\% VQA retention vs.\ 47.2\% grounding retention under 88.9\% token reduction; further pruning studies report the same fragility for pixel and UI grounding~\cite{litelvlm,focusui} (figures from recent preprints; re-verified before submission). The mechanism is intuitive: emitting a correct box requires a preserved global spatial reference frame and fine positional detail; answering ``what color is the dog'' does not.

\paragraph{For quantization, nobody has looked systematically.} The VLM PTQ literature evaluates exclusively on VQA/OCR/chart/doc/reasoning; MBQ~\cite{mbq} frames sensitivity as a \emph{modality} issue, not a \emph{capability} issue---no existing method asks which layers carry \emph{localization}. Direct evidence is two isolated datapoints pointing in opposite directions: GWQ's incidental near-lossless RefCOCO table at ${\sim}4.6$ bits with FP16 outliers retained~\cite{gwq}, vs.\ an applied study's 4-bit Qwen-VL that classifies damage correctly but ``fail[s] consistently'' to localize it~\cite{vehicledamage}. The Qwen3 family is known to degrade steeply at $\le$3 bits~\cite{qwen3quant}; the closest mixed-precision line is component-wise vision/LLM allocation evaluated on MME only~\cite{rethinkingsmall}; SQAP-VLA~\cite{sqapvla} protects visual \emph{input} tokens for robot policies.

\paragraph{Why Qwen3-VL-2B.} Qwen3-VL~\cite{qwen3vl} trains grounding natively and emits coordinates as plain text in a normalized $[0,1000]$ system, and the 2B is edge-realistic (${\sim}1.5$\,GB at W4; we write WxAy for $x$-bit weights, $y$-bit activations). Following~\cite{rethinkingsmall}, the vision tower stays at BF16/INT8; all aggressive quantization targets the LLM.

\section{Measuring the grounding gap}

\paragraph{Pipeline overview.} (1) Freeze hygienic evaluation subsets; (2) quantize the LLM (RTN/GPTQ at W8/W4/W3; W4A8 stress); (3) score grounding, VQA, and hallucination from \emph{decoded} outputs against image-blind floors; (4) profile per-module capability sensitivity with promote-one probes; (5) allocate extra precision greedily under a memory budget; (6) verify against matched-memory random and VQA-driven controls, a held-out OOD set, and full test splits.

\paragraph{Setup and data hygiene.} Reference model $=$ Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct at BF16; quantized model $=$ the same with quantized LLM weights. $D_{\text{probe}}$: ${\sim}512$ RefCOCO-train samples in the chat template, used only for sensitivity profiling (\S\ref{sec:method}). $D_{\text{dev}}$: 1{,}000 RefCOCO-val samples, used \emph{only} for selection---never for calibration or profiling. Reported RefCOCO-val numbers exclude the $D_{\text{dev}}$ samples; the ODinW-13 evaluation set is never touched by calibration, profiling, or selection; so probe, selection, and reported test data are strictly disjoint. RefCOCOg uses the umd split (the google split leaks images between train and val), and $D_{\text{probe}}$ excludes any image appearing in the val/test splits of \emph{any} RefCOCO variant, since the three variants draw on overlapping COCO images.

\paragraph{Evaluation pipeline.} Each REC sample is one image plus one chat-template query---``Locate the \{expression\}, output its \bbox{} in JSON''---decoded greedily ($\le$64 new tokens). The emitted JSON is parsed (regex fallback), mapped $[0,1000] \to$ pixels, and scored by IoU against the ground-truth box: correct at IoU $\ge 0.5$; mean GIoU recorded. Unparseable outputs count as wrong; the parse-failure rate is reported separately (quantization can break the output format itself). The three RefCOCO variants stress different skills (RefCOCO: spatial language; RefCOCO+: attributes only; RefCOCOg: sentence-length descriptions). For ODinW-13, one category per prompt (``locate every \{class\}''), greedy IoU matching, precision/recall@0.5. REC accuracy is additionally stratified by ground-truth box size, where degradation is expected to concentrate.

\paragraph{Detection in the loop.} Every choice a compression pipeline normally makes by perplexity or VQA---group size, bit budget, which modules get promoted---is made here by $D_{\text{dev}}$ grounding metrics (REC acc@0.5 $+$ mean GIoU vs.\ ground-truth boxes). General capability is a \emph{constraint}: VQA and hallucination scores must stay within a pre-registered tolerance (1.5 points) of the same-method text-calibrated quantized baseline, checked by a paired test on a fixed ${\sim}$5k-item VQAv2 subset and POPE. This inversion is itself a claim: the field's selection signal has been blind to detection.

\section{GCQ: localization-sensitivity-guided mixed precision}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Step 1---profiling (forward-only).} Profiling runs in the same direction the allocator spends: start from the \emph{fully quantized} model at the aggressive width (a standard text-calibrated GPTQ/AWQ checkpoint---the base PTQ pipeline is deliberately untouched), then for each LLM module $m$ (attention and MLP per block; ${\sim}2{\times}28 \approx 56$ modules on the 2B) promote \emph{only} $m$ to 8-bit and score the \emph{reduction} in grounding damage on $D_{\text{probe}}$ with a decoding-free proxy:
\begin{equation}
s_m \;=\; \mathrm{KL}_{\text{base}} - \mathrm{KL}_{+m}, \qquad
\mathrm{KL}_{c} \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{D_{\text{probe}}}\Big[\textstyle\sum_{t \in C} \mathrm{KL}\big(p_{\text{ref}}(\cdot \mid x, y_{<t}) \,\|\, p_{c}(\cdot \mid x, y_{<t})\big)\Big],
\end{equation}
where $C$ is the set of coordinate-token positions in the teacher-forced target (the tokens covering digits, commas, and brackets inside \bbox{} arrays; well-defined under multi-digit tokenizer merges). This measures exactly the marginal utility the greedy allocator consumes, at the deployment operating point. The proxy is validated before it is trusted---token-level agreement correlates imperfectly with geometric error~\cite{groundingtokens}---by rank-correlating $s_m$ against decoded REC gains from the same promotions on a module subset; if correlation is poor, profiling falls back to direct decoded REC on a smaller probe.

\paragraph{Step 2---allocation (greedy, two-tier).} Given an average-bits budget $B$, promote the module with the highest $s_m$ per extra byte to 8-bit until the budget is exhausted; embeddings and \texttt{lm\_head} stay 8-bit always (they carry the digit vocabulary). The arithmetic, stated honestly: at $B=4.25$ the budget promotes ${\sim}88$M of the 2B's ${\sim}1.4$B quantizable parameters---about 7 attention modules, or 2--3 MLP modules, or a mix---costing ${\sim}{+}6\%$ of LLM weight memory (${\sim}$50--60\,MB) over uniform W4; $B=4.5$ doubles both. The allocation has only a handful of promotions of resolution: comparisons are between allocation \emph{policies} at matched memory. Known proxy--deployment gaps (per-module isolation, teacher forcing, RTN-for-profiling vs.\ GPTQ-final) are all bounded by one rule: no allocation is accepted on proxy numbers---every candidate is evaluated end-to-end on decoded $D_{\text{dev}}$ metrics; the proxy only orders the search.

\paragraph{What we deliberately do not do.} No calibration-set changes (gains attribute to allocation alone), no training of any kind, no learned allocator, no architecture, tokenizer, or RL changes.

\section{Experimental plan}

\paragraph{Settings.} BF16 reference; W8A16, W4A16, W3A16 via RTN/GPTQ/AWQ; W4A8 as one stress condition; GCQ at $B \in \{4.25, 4.5\}$.

\paragraph{Benchmarks and metrics.} Grounding (primary): RefCOCO/+/g REC acc@0.5 (val/testA/testB; val excludes $D_{\text{dev}}$) and mean GIoU; held-out ODinW-13 subset. General (constraint): VQAv2 subset $+$ POPE. Headline: retention $R = S_{\text{quant}}/S_{\text{BF16}}$ on accuracy-type metrics; because chance floors differ across benchmarks (POPE 50\%, VQA language-prior, REC ${\approx}0$), the headline figure uses floor-corrected $R' = (S_{\text{quant}} - S_{\text{floor}})/(S_{\text{BF16}} - S_{\text{floor}})$ with floors measured by an image-blind pass; raw $R$ and absolute deltas reported alongside.

\paragraph{Preliminary findings (completed).} Six results from the first measurement campaign (Table~\ref{tab:main}, Figures~\ref{fig:main} and~\ref{fig:sens}), all on the frozen subsets above. (1) \emph{No grounding-first asymmetry under weight-only RTN at W4}: floor-corrected retention is POPE 98.5\% $>$ grounding 94.9\% $>$ VQA 92.8\% --- capabilities degrade roughly proportionally, in contrast to token pruning's 95\%-vs-47\% asymmetry; grounding damage is nonetheless real ($-4.4$ points, replicated at $-5.1$ on the disjoint selection set). (2) \emph{Localization sensitivity is concentrated, not diffuse} (H2): the promote-one-module profile over all 56 module-groups peaks in a contiguous band of \emph{attention} modules in layers 10--17 of 28; MLPs lose every per-byte comparison at the B=4.25 budget. (3) \emph{Sensitivity-guided allocation works and is specific}: promoting the band to 8-bit ($+6.25$\% LLM weight memory) recovers 48\% of the lost grounding (84.5 $\to$ 86.6), $5$--$6\times$ the effect of random promotion at an \emph{identical} byte budget ($84.9 \pm 0.3$); B=4.5 recovers 64\%, monotonically. (4) \emph{The constraint holds with margin}: GCQ's VQAv2 is $+0.4$ \emph{above} the uniform-W4 baseline. (5) \emph{The band generalizes out-of-domain}: on a held-out ODinW-13 slice (500 images; aerial/thermal/web, never used in selection), uniform W4 loses 11\% of relative F1 (vs.\ 5\% of in-domain REC) and the RefCOCO-chosen allocation recovers \textbf{61\%} of it. (6) \emph{Everything replicates on the full official test splits} (testA/testB, $n{=}5657/5095$): 49--60\% recovery on testA, 32--43\% on the harder testB; the core effect is significant (paired bootstrap, testA $+1.84$ [1.29, 2.39]). (7) \emph{The pattern replicates across quantizers and activation quantization} (GPTQ-W4 and RTN-W4A8: both proportional) --- but at GPTQ-W3 the model half-survives and the capabilities finally separate: \emph{grounding dies first} (0\% parseable boxes vs.\ 17.7\% partial VQA function). (8) \emph{Sensitivity maps are quantizer-specific} ($\rho$(RTN map, GPTQ map) $=0.082$): allocation must be profiled on the deployed quantizer --- the mismatched map costs $-1.2$, the matched map restores parity and recovers 44\% of lost GIoU with zero parse failures; cruder quantizers convert placement into hit-rate, stronger ones into precision. (9) \emph{The damage is a fine-spatial-precision gradient} (held-out ODinW by ground-truth box size): at W4, small-object recall falls $18.5 \to 8.7$ ($-53\%$), medium $-26\%$, large $\pm0\%$ --- the asymmetry was never absent, it is \emph{invisible to large-object benchmarks} (explaining finding 1), and GCQ recovers 55--58\% of the small/medium loss. H3's $\ge$90\%-recovery bar is not reached training-free at $\le$4.5 bits; the pre-registered escalation (\S\ref{sec:method}) covers the remainder.

\paragraph{Consequences.} Practitioners: a stock 4-bit VLM has silently lost half its small-object detection --- audit before shipping (our harness is that audit); $+6\%$ well-placed memory buys half back at zero VQA cost. Toolchains: profile sensitivity on the deployed quantizer; report grounding on model cards. Research: capabilities are separable, mappable structures --- compression can become capability-aware, and the ${\sim}35\%$ residual ceiling is the target for recovery training.

\paragraph{Baselines and matched controls.} BF16; RTN/GPTQ/AWQ with standard text calibration (status quo); uniform W4; VQA-sensitivity-driven allocation at equal memory; random promotion at equal memory; GCQ. Equal-memory controls ensure any gain is attributable to \emph{where} the bits go, not how many there are.

\paragraph{Ablations.} \textbf{A1} (sensitivity structure): Spearman $\rho$ between $s_m$ and per-module VQA sensitivity---do the two allocations differ? \textbf{A2} (allocation policy): grounding-driven vs.\ uniform vs.\ random vs.\ VQA-driven at matched average bits. \textbf{A3} (generalization): held-out ODinW-13 for an allocation chosen on RefCOCO.

\section{Hypotheses and pre-registered kill criteria}

\textbf{H1 (measurement).} At W4---and clearly at W3 and W4A8---grounding retention trails VQA retention by $>$10 floor-corrected points.
\textbf{H2 (structure).} $s_m$ is non-uniform and only partially correlated with VQA sensitivity, so grounding- and VQA-driven allocations differ materially.
\textbf{H3 (method).} At the most aggressive setting where H1's gap manifests, grounding-driven allocation at $B \le 4.5$ recovers $\ge$90\% of the BF16 grounding lost by the uniform baseline (same quantizer, same calibration), within the 1.5-point VQA tolerance, at the stated ${\sim}$6--12\% LLM-weight-memory premium.

\paragraph{Kill criteria (pre-registered).} Every branch resolved; outcomes reported in findings (1)--(9). The ${\sim}65\%$ training-free ceiling motivates the deferred distillation escalation.

\paragraph{Known limitations.} (i) $s_m$ is teacher-forced and per-module (single-module decoded effects sit below noise at feasible probe sizes), so every accepted configuration is selected end-to-end on decoded metrics and proxy validity rests on the allocation-level controls. (ii) The novelty claim is \emph{to our knowledge} (closest: \cite{gwq,mbq,feather,nuwa,sqapvla,rethinkingsmall}). (iii) Single-model; recent-preprint figures provisional. The campaign is forward-only (${\sim}$50 single-researcher GPU-hours, public data); deliverables: measurements, maps, code, and a checkpoint \emph{whose model card reports grounding}.
